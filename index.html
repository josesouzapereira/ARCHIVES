VOIDEN®
V-001 / 2026
CONCEPT ↓

VOIDEN exists somewhere between streetwear and industrial design. The brief: build an identity that refuses easy categorization. No season. No apology. A digital flagship where pieces exist for a reason — then disappear.

VISUAL LANGUAGE ↓

Heavy type. Maximum contrast. Grain texture. The visual language was assembled from industrial catalogues, underground zines, and late-night concrete — translated into a web experience.
